Personal Stylist Guide & Rules

Core rules used by stylists to transform casual garments into Haute Couture silhouettes.

👑 The 5 Golden Rules of Personal Styling

01

Rule of Thirds (Proportion Balance)

Avoid 50/50 visual balance (half shirt, half pants). Instead, tuck in tops or wear cropped jackets to create a 1/3 top to 2/3 bottom ratio, visually lengthening legs.

🌻
02

Texture Contrast Pairing

Pair contrasting fabric textures to add depth: Leather jacket + Knit sweater, Denim + Silk top, or Heavy wool coat + Crisp cotton shirt.

🌻
03

The Sandwich Method

Match your footwear color with your top layer or headwear color, sandwiching a contrasting bottom piece in the middle for effortless balance.

🌻
04

Shoe-Accessory Anchor Rule

Ensure your belt, footwear, and leather accessories share matching undertones (e.g., cognac shoes with cognac belt; black boots with silver hardware).

🌻
05

Statement Piece Isolation

If wearing a bold pattern or vibrant hue, keep all other outfit elements neutral to allow the statement piece to shine without visual clutter.
